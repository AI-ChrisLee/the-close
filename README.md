# The Close: install in 60 seconds

The part of an Execution Squad that runs the money conversation. The Winning Offer built your
offer document; this one sells it. It builds ONE file, `squad/sales.md`, your sales script:
what you say, what it costs, the one win you use as proof, the 13 questions that get a buyer to
price their own problem, and every objection real buyers have already thrown at you with the
answer that moved them. Then it grades every call the moment it ends and writes what the call
taught back into the script. Call six is better than call one.

## What to bring

Your offer document at `squad/business.md` (the Winning Offer builds it from your warm calls or
the market; one with no confirmed stamp yet is enough to start). Then 5 things only you know:
your booking link, your payment link, what delivery looks like in days, the way you say your
price out loud, and one win you can name with how it happened ("none yet" is an answer for the
booking link, the payment link and the win). Replies can be sorted before any of that exists; a price cannot be quoted before it
does.

## Install

Drop this whole folder, `references/` included, into `.claude/skills/` in your business folder,
then quit and reopen Claude Code. No connector. Google Drive, if you connected it in g2, gets a
copy of the script as a Google Doc.

## Run it

Four modes, four sentences:

- **"Build my sales document."** Five questions, then the script, the client page rendered
  from it, and a stop for your yes on the price line and the plan. Once.
- **"I just got off a call with [name]."** Paste the transcript, drop the recording's path, or
  give your notes. It grades the hour against 4 gates with the evidence quoted, names the one
  thing to fix, files the debrief, updates your script with what the call taught, and writes
  the pipeline row.
- **"Update the script with this: ..."** and the line changes, with your yes when it is one you
  say out loud.
- **"Sort my replies"** with the batch pasted, and every reply lands in one of five buckets
  with a draft under it.

Stopped halfway, or closed the laptop? Say **"continue the Close"** in a new window. It reads
what is on disk and picks up at the first thing missing.

## What you get

One script that gets sharper every call, one client-facing page, one folder per buyer holding
their words and every call's grade, and a pipeline where every row says the truth about the
money. With Google Drive connected (g2) the script also lands in your Google Docs and the link
is printed; without it, it stays on disk and the run says one line about it. It never sends
anything: it drafts, and your hand presses send. Signed plus paid is the only thing that makes
a client.
